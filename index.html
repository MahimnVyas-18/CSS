RAMA

Childhood

technology Posted on: October 2,2024

Lord Rama was born in Ayodhya to King Dasharatha and Queen Kaushalya. As a child, Rama was gentle, obedient, and full of compassion.
He showed great respect for elders and deep love for his brothers.
Rama learned archery and scriptures at a young age and excelled in both.
Even in childhood, his calm nature and sense of duty made everyone admire him.
Rama was known for his truthfulness and never spoke harsh words.
He treated everyone equally, whether rich or poor.
Rama loved spending time with his brothers—Lakshmana, Bharata, and Shatrughna.
From a young age, he showed bravery along with kindness.
He listened carefully to his teachers and followed their guidance.
People of Ayodhya loved Rama for his polite behavior.
His childhood reflected the qualities of an ideal son and prince.
